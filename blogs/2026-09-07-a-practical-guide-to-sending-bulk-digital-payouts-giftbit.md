Organizations use digital disbursements to move money and monetary value to many people at once, for things like research incentives, rebates, and vendor payouts. They've always been a useful tool, and in 2025 the U.S. government put even more weight behind them with Executive Order 14247, which requires the Treasury to stop mailing most federal payments as paper checks.
