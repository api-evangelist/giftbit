Knowing how to send global rewards and incentives to people in other countries requires more than collecting a few foreign email addresses and setting some gift card values. A digital reward that works in one country may not work in another, and currency, brand availability, denominations, and redemption rules can all vary by market. Whether you're sending a thank-you to a teammate for a job well done or bulk gift cards to international research participants, start with where your recipients live, then match the reward, currency, and delivery setup to each location.
