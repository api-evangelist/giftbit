TL;DR: Grocery gift cards can be a great holiday gift for employees, as long as they’re easy to use. Give people some choice, send them early enough for holiday shopping, and include a genuine thank-you. For distributed teams, offering local options is usually easier than trying to find one grocery chain that works for everyone.
