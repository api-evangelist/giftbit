Gift cards for remote employees can be easy to send, easy to personalize, and easy for your people to actually use, but only if you have the right tools. Whether your remote/hybrid team is scattered across time zones, countries, or even just a city or two, recognition still needs to feel personal and thoughtful. That means choosing gifts employees can redeem where they live for brands they really want (ideally without creating extra admin work for you or running up a large bill for your Finance team, either).
